# Onderzoek 2: Solliciteren in Vlaanderen (2026)

Language note: this file is in English because it is internal working material; everything that goes out to employers is in Dutch and uses "u". Source pages of VDAB, antwerpen.be, werkenvoor.be, ACV and Sociare could not be loaded directly in this environment; their content comes from search-result extracts. The Vlaamse overheid selection regulations (selectiereglementen 2024-2026) were read in full. Salary figures marked "indicative" must be verified before the app shows them as fact.

## 1. CV conventions in Flanders

- Length: 1 to 2 pages. VDAB: "Gebruik korte, eenvoudige zinnen. Vermijd jargon en afkortingen. Probeer alles op 1 tot 2 pagina's te krijgen."
- Order VDAB recommends: Persoonlijke gegevens (naam, adres, telefoon, e-mail grouped, then nationaliteit, geboortedatum en -plaats, without labels like "Naam:"), a "korte voorstelling" of max 5 lines (profiel), Werkervaring (reverse chronological), Opleiding (most recent first), then Vaardigheden, Talen, Rijbewijs, Interesses/Extra (cursussen, attesten, vrijwilligerswerk).
- Geboortedatum: still normal in Flanders; VDAB lists it among persoonlijke gegevens. For a 20-year-old with 5 years of experience it is an asset, so include it.
- Foto: VDAB says add one only if the employer asks or for a job fair; otherwise optional, neutral background. Government forms use a standardised online form, no photo.
- Career switch (VDAB carrièreswitch pages): put an "Over mij" block of max 5 sentences under the contact details explaining the switch, what attracts you and your strengths; place Vaardigheden above Werkervaring and Opleiding; back skills with a concrete example.
- Trade qualifications, Flemish wording: "Diploma secundair onderwijs, BSO Lassen-constructie" (or TSO Mechanische technieken), "7de specialisatiejaar / Se-n-Se", "duaal leren", "Getuigschrift VCA-basis (Basisveiligheid)", "Lascertificaten EN ISO 9606-1 (TIG 141, MIG/MAG 131/135), aluminium EN ISO 9606-2", "Rijbewijs B". List all valid certificates with their standard, list safety attesten, describe techniques, materials and sectors per job with concrete projects. Never write "MBO" or "middelbaar" (Netherlands terms).
- Europass: accepted, mainly by public services; not the default Flemish layout. A clean self-made PDF is the norm.
- Files: PDF; "Voornaam_Achternaam_CV.pdf" and "Voornaam_Achternaam_Motivatiebrief.pdf".

Flemish vs Netherlands vocabulary: job (BE) not baan (NL); solliciteren voor een functie; secundair onderwijs (BE) not middelbaar/voortgezet onderwijs; BSO/TSO/ASO not vmbo/mbo/havo; interimkantoor not uitzendbureau; brutoloon not brutosalaris; maaltijdcheques, ecocheques, hospitalisatieverzekering, eindejaarspremie, vakantiegeld; loonbrief or loonfiche not loonstrookje; leefloon not bijstand; "wedde" in the public sector; "werfreserve", "aanwerving", "in dienst treden". Use "u" and "uw" for employers.

## 2. Motivatiebrief (sollicitatiebrief)

- Length: 1 A4. VDAB: "Zet alles op 1 pagina." Ik-vorm, active short sentences, no jargon.
- Structure: aanhef; inleiding referring to the vacancy (title, where found, reference) with a personal hook; "waarom deze organisatie"; "waarom ik" (2 or 3 troeven with proof); afsluiting requesting an interview ("Ik licht mijn kandidatuur graag toe in een gesprek."); slotformule; name.
- Aanhef: with a named contact "Geachte mevrouw Peeters," / "Geachte heer De Smet,"; unknown recipient: "Geachte mevrouw, geachte heer,". "Beste" is acceptable for informal ateliers and small firms whose vacancy uses "je". "Geachte" pairs with "Met vriendelijke groeten" (or the very formal "Hoogachtend"); "Beste" pairs only with "Met vriendelijke groeten".
- u vs je: use "u" consistently towards the employer, even if the vacancy says "je". Mirror the vacancy only in tone.
- Signature block: Voornaam Achternaam, straat nummer, postcode gemeente, gsm-nummer, e-mail, optional portfolio link. "Bijlagen: cv, portfolio".
- Email subject: "Sollicitatie [functietitel] - [Voornaam Achternaam]" plus "(ref. XXXX)" if there is one; attach both PDFs; short mail body with the letter as attachment, or the letter as body with CV attached.

## 3. Spontane sollicitatie

Common and expected in Flanders, especially with SMEs, ateliers and cultural houses. VDAB rules: know exactly which job you want, tailor letter and CV to the company, put "spontane sollicitatie" in the subject line, state why this company and what you want to do, put your main troeven at the top of the CV. Find the right person via the website, LinkedIn, a phone call asking who handles spontane sollicitaties, or hand it in personally (very accepted for ateliers). Tone: formal-warm, "u", one page, concrete offer (proefdag or werkbezoek).

## 4. Follow-up etiquette

- After sending: wait 10 to 14 days; one polite mail or one phone call. Do not chase more than once.
- After an interview: ask at the end when to expect an answer, then respect that date. A bedankmail is not very customary in Belgium but is welcomed; send within 24 to 48 hours, thank, recap one point, confirm motivation.
- Rejection: ask feedback, preferably by phone within 24 to 48 hours; ask for sterktes and werkpunten; ask to be kept in mind for future vacancies or the werfreserve.

## 5. Applying to government

Levels and diploma: niveau A = master, B = bachelor, C = diploma secundair onderwijs (Stad Antwerpen: "diploma van het middelbaar onderwijs of 6de of 7de jaar beroepsonderwijs"), D and E = no diploma. A welder with a BSO diploma qualifies for niveau C; without a diploma for niveau D.

Vlaamse overheid (werkenvoorvlaanderen.be):
- Apply only via the online sollicitatieformulier (standaard-cv plus motivation and competency questions), only in Dutch. Diplomas obtained after 2002 in Flanders are checked in the LED-databank.
- Alternative access to niveau C without diploma: a VDAB toegangsbewijs for the function, an ervaringsbewijs, or a bewijs van beroepskwalificatie at the level of the vacancy. Niveau B via a niveaubewijs (niveautest) with 4 years of relevant experience.
- Steps: cv-screening; voorselectie (computer test plus standard questionnaire on motivatie, kennis, zorgvuldigheid; eliminating); screening competenties (interview, personality questionnaire); functiespecifieke screening: jury interview on motivation, vision, knowledge, skills. For D technical jobs: practical tests. Others join the wervingsreserve, valid 1 or 2 years, also usable for similar vacancies.
- Statute: since 1 June 2024 new recruits get a contract of onbepaalde duur. Pay scales NC121, ND121; the line manager decides whether prior experience is valorised. 35 vakantiedagen plus free days between Christmas and New Year, maaltijdcheques, hospitalisatieverzekering, free public transport or fietsvergoeding.
- Standard competencies: verantwoordelijkheid nemen, flexibiliteit, samenwerken, klantgerichtheid, plannen en organiseren, zorgvuldigheid. Prepare a STARR example for each.
- Expect: rijbewijs B, medical fitness for D, uittreksel strafregister.
- Agentschap Onroerend Erfgoed recruits via werkenvoorvlaanderen.be (depot jobs at C121 asked only a diploma hoger secundair). KMSKA: CV plus motivatiebrief to sollicitaties@kmska.be.

Stad Antwerpen (job.antwerpen.be): cv-screening on CV and motivation; every selection includes a competency interview; often a praktische proef; many selections produce a werfreserve, valid a year for similar vacancies at the same level, leading to a contract of onbepaalde duur. Title for the welder-type job: "C1-Technisch assistent" or D-level vakman roles.

Federal (werkenvoor.be; KIK-IRPA is federal): online CV on werkenvoor.be; niveau C needs a diploma hoger secundair, niveau D none; generic screening (abstract reasoning, situational judgement) then function-specific screening and interview; reserves.

## 6. Interview conventions

- STARR (Situatie, Taak, Actie, Resultaat, Reflectie) is the standard technique. Expect: "Waarom deze switch?", "Wat weet u over onze organisatie?", "Hoe combineert u werk en studie?", "Waar ziet u zichzelf over vijf jaar?".
- Dress: neat, matching the workplace; ateliers and museums smart-casual; never work clothes. Arrive 5 to 10 minutes early.
- Salary: do not raise it in the first interview; if asked, give a bruto maandloon range. Government pay is on fixed scales, so ask how prior experience will be valorised.
- Leaving because of fatigue: no obligation to disclose; health questions are not allowed. Safe framing: "Na vijf jaar industrieel laswerk ben ik toe aan een nieuwe uitdaging waarin ik mijn vakmanschap kan inzetten voor erfgoed en ontwerp." Never use the word burn-out. If pressed: "Ik heb bewust de tijd genomen om mijn richting te bepalen en ben volledig klaar om te starten."
- Studying alongside work is normal and seen positively; state it upfront with the concrete schedule and show how it feeds the job.

## 7. Portfolio for ateliers and museums

8 to 15 pages, under 10 MB, 6 to 10 werkstukken, each with title, year, material, technique (TIG/MIG, aluminium, staal, hout), your role, one strong photo and one detail; drawings or 3D for interior work; short "Over mij" and contact page. Link in the CV under its own heading, name one or two pieces in the letter, bring a printed version to the interview.

## 8. Legal and etiquette points

Cao nr. 38 obliges employers to ask only job-relevant questions; the antidiscriminatiewet of 10 May 2007 protects health (current, future and past), handicap, age, origin, belief and syndicale overtuiging. Employers may not ask about health or union membership. Do not mention union membership or health in CV or letter; no GDPR clause is needed on a Belgian CV.

## 9. Salary conventions

Belgians talk in brutoloon per maand, effectively 13,92 months (12 months plus eindejaarspremie plus dubbel vakantiegeld). Standard extralegale voordelen 2026: maaltijdcheques max €10 per gewerkte dag, ecocheques €250 per jaar, hospitalisatieverzekering, groepsverzekering, fietsvergoeding (€0,32 per km from 1 October 2026), woon-werkverkeer.
- PC 111 (metaalbouw, arbeiders): minimum hourly wages rose on 1 January 2026 and were indexed by 2,81 percent on 1 July 2026; 38-hour week. Indicative: a certified TIG/MIG welder in Antwerp region earns €17 to €22 per hour, about €2.900 to €3.600 bruto per maand (verify against the 2026 barema).
- PC 329.01 (socio-cultureel): barema's indexed by 2 percent on 1 February 2026; a technical or atelier worker falls in L-schalen or B1c; indicative start €2.400 to €2.700 bruto with eindejaarspremie.
- Vlaamse overheid: indicative 2026 starts D1 about €2.300 to €2.500, C1 about €2.500 to €2.700 bruto, plus 35 vakantiedagen and benefits (verify with the salarissimulator).
- Stad Antwerpen (sourced): C1-technisch assistent full-time minimum €2.460 bruto with no experience, €2.769 with 5 years of relevant experience, €2.967 with 10 years; free hospitalisatieverzekering incl. tandzorg, maaltijdcheques, fietsvergoeding, aanvullend pensioen, eindejaarstoelage.
- Realistic expectation for Remi: €2.700 to €3.200 bruto in a specialist metal atelier, €2.500 to €2.800 bruto entry-level in culture, heritage or government (niveau C with 5 years valorised).

Bedankt voor uw bericht en voor de tijd die u in mijn kandidatuur hebt geïnvesteerd. Het spijt me dat ik niet de weerhouden kandidaat ben, maar ik respecteer uw beslissing. Om mij verder te ontwikkelen zou ik het erg waarderen als u mij kort kunt meegeven welke punten de doorslag hebben gegeven en waar u mijn sterktes zag. Mag ik u ook vragen mijn kandidatuur in gedachten te houden voor gelijkaardige vacatures of een toekomstige werfreserve? Ik wens u en uw team veel succes met de nieuwe collega.
Met vriendelijke groeten
[Voornaam Achternaam], [gsm]
