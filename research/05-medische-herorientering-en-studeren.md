## Route A: unemployment benefit plus full-time study (VDAB vrijstelling)

- Access requires involuntary unemployment (see onderzoek 1). Amount 2026: 65 percent of capped gross wage for 3 months, then 60 percent; for a gross wage around €2.800 roughly €1.600 to €1.800 per month at the start [estimate].
- The 24-month limit and the training exception: a jobseeker who, before the 24 months lapse, starts a VDAB-approved knelpuntopleiding (including OKOT) or studies with vrijstelling keeps benefits until the end of that training, with a maximum extension believed to be 2 additional years [UNVERIFIED]. Whether non-knelpunt studies also extend is not confirmed. Onderzoek 1 found the RVA T170 rule: a full-time VDAB-approved beroepsopleiding of at least 3 months started after 1 January 2026 extends the right by up to 12 months. Ask VDAB and ACV for the written rule.
- Types of vrijstelling: (1) beroepsopleiding with a VDAB-opleidingscontract: automatic; (2) OKOT; (3) studies met volledig leerplan (dag graduaat, bachelor, master): requested and approved by VDAB before the start; historically at least 312 uitkeringsdagen (about a year) before the start unless the study prepares for a knelpuntberoep or you have no diploma secundair; study must be higher than your current diploma; renewed yearly on study progress [UNVERIFIED for 2026]; (4) trainings under 20 hours per week or in the evening: no vrijstelling needed, stay available, report to VDAB. Remi's current evening bachelor falls under (4).
- OKOT: VDAB contract for a graduaat, bachelor or secundair volwassenenonderwijs programme leading to a knelpuntberoep; conditions: registered with VDAB, no diploma of the same level, positive intake, programme on the OKOT list, start at the beginning of the academic year; VDAB pays inschrijvingsgeld and material, reimburses travel; benefits kept for the normal duration [medium]. Programmes typically on the list (2024-2025 memory): educatieve graduaatsopleiding and educatieve bachelor secundair onderwijs for knelpuntvakken (technische en praktijkvakken), graduaat Bouwkundig tekenen, graduaat Werforganisatie, graduaat Elektromechanische systemen, graduaat Orthopedagogische begeleiding, verpleegkunde [UNVERIFIED for 2026].
- Premies: verplaatsingsvergoeding, kinderopvang, a stimulanspremie for long-term jobseekers in a knelpuntopleiding [UNVERIFIED amounts].
- Student jobs and flexi-jobs during Route A: flexi-jobs are reserved for people employed at least 4/5 elsewhere; studentenarbeid requires student status as main occupation; any paid day goes on the controlekaart. Route A income is the benefit alone.
- Request via the VDAB bemiddelaar and Mijn Loopbaan with inschrijvingsbewijs and lessenrooster, before the start date. ACV files the claim and pays.

## Route B: medical reorientation via the mutualiteit (RIZIV)

- Access: incapacity starting during employment: gewaarborgd loon (arbeiders degressive over 30 days), then the ziekenfonds from day 31 [stable]. Dismissal while sick is allowed with notice or indemnity; the uitkering continues after the contract ends because the incapacity started while insured; incapacity starting within 30 days after losing that status is still covered [medium].
- Amounts: primaire arbeidsongeschiktheid (months 1 to 12): 60 percent of the capped gross wage, maximum around €2.700 per month [UNVERIFIED]; minima for "regelmatig werknemer" from month 7 (alleenstaande roughly €1.900) [UNVERIFIED]. From month 13 (invaliditeit): 55 percent alleenstaande, 65 percent gezinshoofd, 40 percent samenwonende [stable].
- Assessment: at least 66 percent loss of earning capacity; the first 6 months measured against the last profession (welder), from month 7 against all professions compatible with training and experience (referentieberoep rule) [stable]. A welder who could plausibly do CAD, teaching or workshop work risks being declared fit at month 7 unless a re-integratie or herscholing programme is agreed. Burnout is certified by the huisarts through functional limitations; the adviserend arts uses a questionnaire around week 4 and a restcapaciteit assessment around week 10 [medium]; reports from a psychologist and from the arbeidsarts strengthen the file. The physical unsustainability of welding (musculoskeletal, respiratory) should be documented too. A mild burnout alone rarely justifies long incapacity.
- Socioprofessionele re-integratie / beroepsherscholing (art. 109bis ZIV-wet): with the agreement of the adviserend arts, prepared with the Terug Naar Werk-coördinator, a programme may include oriëntatie, an opleiding and a stage. Accepted: any recognised training (VDAB, volwassenenonderwijs, SYNTRA, graduaat or bachelor) if realistic and time-limited; a 2-year graduaat is easier to get approved than a 3-year bachelor [UNVERIFIED]. Financial (2024-2025 figures, [medium]): inschrijvingsgeld, material and travel paid; premie €5 per effectively followed lesson hour; €500 on successful completion; recognition of incapacity maintained during the programme and 6 months after, without reassessment against referentieberoepen. Application through the adviserend arts of the ziekenfonds, who drafts the programme.
- Terug Naar Werk-coördinator (every ziekenfonds since 2022) builds the trajectory with VDAB, GTB or loopbaanbegeleiders [stable]. The Terug Naar Werk-fonds (since April 2024) gives a budget of up to €1.800 for specialised guidance or training [medium].
- Re-integratietraject with the arbeidsarts (Codex welzijn, revised 2022): the worker may request it at any time; decision A (temporarily unfit, adapted work), B (definitively unfit for the agreed work, other work possible) or C; the employer must produce a plan or a motivated report [stable]. Medische overmacht is a separate procedure, since 1 January 2026 possible after 6 months of uninterrupted incapacity (onderzoek 1), contract ends without opzegvergoeding, the employer pays €1.800 into the fund, the worker keeps the ziekte-uitkering. For Remi, requesting a traject with the externe dienst arbeidsarts is low-risk, and a decision B is the strongest document to open the herscholing route.
- Progressieve werkhervatting (toegelaten arbeid): with prior authorisation of the adviserend arts and at least 50 percent remaining incapacity, part-time work is allowed while keeping a benefit reduced only for the work volume above 20 percent (a 50 percent job cuts the benefit by 30 percent) [stable]. Combined with part-time study this is the realistic "study while medically reorienting" configuration. Report any study to the adviserend arts.

## Route C: study while working part-time (relevant for the next job)

- Tijdskrediet met motief opleiding (CAO 103): 24 months seniority with the employer; training of at least 360 hours or 27 studiepunten per year; full-time, half-time or 1/5; maximum 36 months; RVA allowance roughly €600 to €800 full-time, €300 to €400 half-time, €200 to €260 for 1/5 [UNVERIFIED]; Flemish top-up Vlaams opleidingskrediet [UNVERIFIED].
- Vlaams opleidingsverlof: see onderzoek 4 (125 hours, reform 1 September 2026).
- Opleidingscheques: max €250 per year, half subsidised, for workers without a higher-education diploma. Individuele leerrekening: pilot announced for 2026 [UNVERIFIED]. KMO-portefeuille: companies only.
- Loopbaancheques: 7 years of experience required; Remi is not eligible; alternatives: VDAB oriëntatietraject for jobseekers (free), ACV loopbaanbegeleiding, Leerwinkel / Huis van het Leren Antwerpen.

## Suggested sequencing

1. Now, while employed: huisarts visit to document fatigue and physical strain; request a re-integratietraject with the arbeidsarts; collect psychologist report if relevant; check Vlaams opleidingsverlof for the current KASKA study.
2. ACV dienst werkloosheid and ACV-CSC METEA review the exit scenarios (ontslag door werkgever, ziekte, tijdskrediet).
3. If leaving via unemployment: register with VDAB before the academic year; request OKOT on the educatief graduaat SO or graduaat Bouwkundig tekenen; this also protects against the 2-year limit.
4. If leaving via incapacity: ask the Terug Naar Werk-coördinator for a herscholing programme toward a 2-year graduaat, using progressieve werkhervatting for part-time atelier or teaching income.
