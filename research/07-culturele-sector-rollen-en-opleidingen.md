## 3. What to learn, ranked

1. Safety and rigging certificates (weeks): VCA-basis, IPAF hoogtewerker, BA4/BA5, heftruck, STEPP rigging basis and veilig werken op hoogte, ETTE certificate. €100 to €600 each; VDAB pays for jobseekers; Sociaal Fonds Podiumkunsten reimburses PC 304 employers. Turns a welder into a hireable stage or depot technician within months. stepp.be, ette.eu, ipaf.org, besacc-vca.be.
2. FARO Basiscursus behoud en beheer and depot courses (days spread over weeks, €100 to €300): the standard entry ticket to collectiezorg; depotwijzer.be is a free knowledge base. faro.be/vorming.
3. Se-n-Se Podiumtechnieken (7th year TSO) or duaal variant: one school year with placements; free; fastest formal stage qualification (Antwerp providers UNVERIFIED, list on onderwijskiezer.be).
4. Professionele bachelor Podiumtechnieken, RITCS (Erasmushogeschool Brussel, campus Bottelarij): 3 years, no artistic entrance exam (medium confidence); about €1.180 per year; OKOT possible if podiumtechnicus is on the knelpuntlijst that year (UNVERIFIED).
5. Educatief graduaat secundair onderwijs (AP, KdG, Thomas More): 90 ECTS, 1,5 to 3 years evening or blended; admission with secundair diploma plus 3 years of relevant experience; OKOT-eligible (UNVERIFIED for 2026).
6. UAntwerpen Bachelor and Master Conservatie-Restauratie (Mutsaard): 3 plus 2 years full-time daytime, option metaal (list UNVERIFIED); this agent believes there is a toelatingsproef in spring or summer, while onderzoek 4 read that anyone with a secondary diploma can enrol. To verify directly with UAntwerpen.
7. SYNTRA evening courses: Decorbouwer, Podium- en eventtechnicus, Kunstsmid (campuses UNVERIFIED), 1 to 2 years, €500 to €1.500 per year.
8. Design route: finish the current study; later Master Interieurarchitectuur (UAntwerpen, KU Leuven Sint-Lucas, UHasselt) via schakelprogramma; scenography masters only outside Flemish higher education (La Cambre Brussels in French; HKU Utrecht, Zuyd Maastricht in English). No confirmed Master Scenografie in Flanders.
9. CVO Antwerp evening courses (Encora, Vitant): lassen gevorderd, interieurbouwer, meubelmaker, meubelrestauratie; smeden at DKO academies (Berchem, Deurne, Hoboken).

Not found: a graduaat podiumtechnieken, "Fabriek Podium", a VDAB course literally called podiumtechnicus (VDAB has periodically run stage-technician training with the Sociaal Fonds and SYNTRA, UNVERIFIED for 2026-2027).

## 4. Internships, traineeships and first steps

Warning repeated from onderzoek 1: the VDAB instruments require a registered jobseeker with benefits, which means an exit via dismissal, not resignation and not a mutual agreement. Instruments: Beroepsverkennende stage (1 to 30 days at any employer while keeping benefits; ideal first door: a week in the Opera Ballet ateliers or the KMSKA depot), Werkervaringsstage (1 to 6 months, small allowance plus benefits), IBO (1 to 6 months, employer commits to hire; the classic way small cultural teams hire a technician they like), Beroepsinlevingsstage (BIS: a contract directly with an employer without VDAB, minimum allowance around €1.000 per month UNVERIFIED, widely used in Flemish arts organisations; Cultuurloket explains it), duaal leren. No paid technical trainee programme at the Antwerp or Flemish museums is known (UNVERIFIED).

Support organisations: Cultuurloket (free advice on contracts, statutes, BIS), Kunstenpunt, oKo (CAO Podiumkunsten), Sociare (329.01 scales), Sociaal Fonds Podiumkunsten (training catalogue, subsidies, loopbaanbegeleiding for PC 304), STEPP (technicians' network, jobs, courses), FARO, Herita.

Volunteering as door opener: Erfgoeddag (18 April 2027, confirmed in onderzoek 3), Open Monumentendag (12 September 2027), Museumnacht Antwerpen, Zomer van Antwerpen seasonal technical crews, Het Theaterfestival (early September), Kunstendag voor Kinderen (Sunday 15 November 2026). Museum vrienden associations and Middelheimmuseum installation days suit an introvert better than front-of-house shifts.
