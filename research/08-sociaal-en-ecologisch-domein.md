## How the sector is organised (Known, details to verify)
- PC 319.01 (Vlaamse opvoedings- en huisvestingsinrichtingen: bijzondere jeugdzorg, VAPH dagcentra, leefgroepen). Function classes: opvoeder-begeleider klasse 1 = bachelor (barema B1c); klasse 2A = diploma secundair in a relevant social direction (B2a); klasse 2B = diploma secundair without relevant direction (B2b); klasse 3 = no secondary diploma (B3). A begeleider job does not legally require a bachelor; the employer decides which class the vacancy opens. Remi would start as klasse 2B unless the vacancy asks klasse 1. Indicative gross starts (UNVERIFIED): B1c about €2.950 to €3.100; B2a €2.700 to €2.850; B2b €2.600 to €2.750; B3 €2.450 to €2.600, plus eindejaarspremie and shift premiums.
- PC 329.01 (socioculturele sector: jeugdwerk, opleiding en tewerkstelling vzw's such as Arktos, Groep INTRO, JES): same lettered scales; vacancies say "bachelor of gelijkwaardig door ervaring"; no legal diploma requirement for jeugdwerker.
- PC 327.01 (Vlaamse maatwerkbedrijven): monitors and werkvloerbegeleiders are omkaderingspersoneel; no diploma requirement; technical mastery plus coaching attitude; indicative €2.600 to €3.000.
- PC 330 (hospitals incl. psychiatric): IFIC pay.
- NAFT (naadloos flexibel traject, formerly time-out) is financed by the Department of Education and run by youth-work vzw's; hands-on, daytime; experience-based hiring is common.

## 1. Youth work and youth training (Antwerp)
- Groep INTRO: opleiding, begeleiding en tewerkstelling of vulnerable groups, incl. Antwerp: NAFT, vooropleidingen and werkplekleren in bouw, hout, metaal en lassen. Roles: instructeur metaal of bouw, werkvloerbegeleider, NAFT-begeleider. Diploma secundair usually sufficient for instructeur. https://www.groepintro.be. Fit 5: the closest match.
- Profo vzw (Antwerpen): training centre for youth and young adults, vooropleidingen and werkplekleren in bouw, hout, metaal (lassen), horeca, logistiek. Roles: instructeur lassen of metaal, begeleider leerwerkplaats. https://www.profo.be. Fit 5.
- Arktos: expertise centre for youth 6 to 25 in vulnerable situations; NAFT with hands-on ateliers; teams in Antwerpen, Mechelen, Turnhout. Roles: NAFT-begeleider, atelierbegeleider. Bachelor often asked or equivalent experience. https://www.arktos.be. Fit 4.
- JES vzw (Antwerpen): stadslabo for children and youth; JES Werkt (vooropleidingen, werkervaring, jobcoaching); check whether Antwerp has a technical atelier. https://www.jes.be. Fit 4.
- Kras Jeugdwerk: youth work in ten Antwerp neighbourhoods; jeugdwerker roles, often part-time and evening. https://www.krasjeugdwerk.be. Fit 3.
- Formaat (jeugdhuizen): jeugdhuiswerker with infrastructure tasks; evening hours. https://formaat.be. Fit 2,5.
- Emino (Antwerpen): specialised training, guidance and placement for people with a disability or health issue; jobcoach, werkvloerbegeleider. https://www.emino.be. Fit 3.
- De Werkplekarchitecten: network of about twenty opleiding en begeleiding vzw's; job board and map. https://www.werkplekarchitecten.be.
- Bijzondere jeugdzorg: Jeugdzorg Emmaüs Antwerpen, Elegast, De Touter: begeleider dagcentrum (klasse 1 or 2), leefgroep (shifts, clashes with study). Fit 2,5 to 3.
- Lejo (Gent-centred), Habbekrats, Uit De Marge (steunpunt with sector job board). "De Werf Antwerpen" and "Stadsmakers" could not be identified as youth-work employers.
Titles to search: technisch begeleider, werkvloerbegeleider, atelierbegeleider, instructeur, praktijkbegeleider, jobcoach, jeugdwerker.

## 2. Social economy (maatwerk)
- Levanto vzw (Antwerpen): largest Antwerp social-economy group: Bouw en Renovatie (renovating social housing with disadvantaged workers), Fietspunt (Antwerpen-Centraal, Berchem), Groen, Wijkwerken, Zorg. Roles: werkvloerbegeleider of instructeur bouw, begeleider fietspunt, technisch instructeur. https://www.levanto.be. Fit 5.
- De Kringwinkel Antwerpen: about ten reuse shops plus sorting, repair, furniture atelier, textile, bikes; Herwin network. Roles: werkbegeleider meubelatelier of herstelatelier. No diploma requirement. https://www.kringwinkelantwerpen.be. Fit 4.
- Manus Antwerpen (groen, renovatie, verhuis, klusdienst), Aralea (Brasschaat: groen, natuurbeheer for Natuurpunt and ANB, houtatelier; small field teams), WerKanT, De Brug (Merksem), Natuurwerk, Ecoso (Mechelen: textile, bikes, wood, reuse of building materials). Fit 3 to 3,5.
- Fietsenwerk: network of social bike points; fietshersteller is a knelpuntberoep, low physical load. https://www.fietsenwerk.be. Fit 3.
- Benchmarks outside Antwerp: Ateljee and Ryhove (Gent), Mariasteen (Gits, large metaalafdeling), Sowepo, Werkmmaat.
- Koepels and boards: Herwin (sociale circulaire ondernemers: kringwinkels, energiesnoeiers, fietspunten), Groep Maatwerk, socialeeconomie.be (Flemish government portal). Arbeidszorg begeleiders via CAW, psychiatric care or maatwerk; search via desocialekaart.be.
- Monitor training: short courses via Herwin, Groep Maatwerk, VIVO, some SYNTRA campuses (UNVERIFIED 2026-27).

## 3. Ecological and circular organisations
- Rotor DC (Anderlecht): deconstruction, cleaning and resale of reclaimed building materials; sister of the Rotor design and research collective. Roles: ontmantelaar, ateliermedewerker (clean, repair, sort), documentation. https://rotordc.com, https://rotordb.org. Fit 4: design plus hands-on plus circular purpose; deconstruction is heavy and it is a Brussels commute, so aim for the atelier side.
- Opalis: directory of reclaimed-material dealers (find Antwerp and Flemish dealers). https://opalis.eu.
- BC materials (Brussels): excavated earth turned into leem building materials; production atelier plus workshops. Fit 3,5.
- Natuurpunt (HQ Mechelen): terreinploegen, technical roles; kettingzaag certificate via Inverde. Fit 3.
- Regionale Landschappen (de Voorkempen, Rivierenland, Kleine en Grote Nete), Bosgroepen: mostly bachelor project staff. Fit 2.
- EcoHuis Antwerpen (Stad, Borgerhout): advice, workshops, demonstration house; C-level posts via the city. Fit 3.
- Energiesnoeiers (Herwin network): social-economy teams doing energy scans and small energy renovations in low-income homes; trained on the job. https://www.energiesnoeiers.net. Fit 3,5.
- Repair&Share (Repair Cafés), Maakbaar Leuven: mostly volunteer; good CV-building. Fablabs and makerspaces (Fablab Factory Antwerp, AP and KdG fablabs, Timelab Gent): occasional technician posts. Fit 3.
- VIBE vzw and ecobouwers.be: directory of bio-ecological builders (strobouw, leembouw, hergebruik) to find small eco-construction firms. Kamp C (Westerlo, provincial sustainable building centre). Vlaanderen Circulair (partner vacancies, UNVERIFIED).
- KMDA (Zoo Antwerpen, Planckendael): in-house technical service building and maintaining enclosures; welding a real asset. Fit 3,5. Stad Antwerpen Stadsbeheer city workshops. Fit 3.
- Cooperatives (Ecopower, Wooncoop, Commons Lab, Boeren en Buren): few technical jobs. An Antwerp materials bank could not be confirmed (Kanaal is an art site; BlueCity is Rotterdam).

## 4. Social care with ateliers
- ZAS (former ZNA plus GZA) psychiatry: Stuivenberg/Cadix, Hoge Beuken, UKJA: begeleider dagactiviteiten of atelier, ergo-assistent, technisch medewerker; IFIC pay. Fit 3.
- Emmaüs psychiatric hospitals: PZ Bethanië (Zoersel), Multiversum (Boechout, Mortsel), PZ Duffel: arbeidszorg and creative or technical ateliers (hout, fiets, tuin). Fit 3,5.
- VAPH services with ateliers: Monnikenheide-Spectrum (Zoersel), Ritmica (Hove: hout, kaarsen, semi-industrial ateliers), OLO-Rotonde (Brasschaat, Antwerpen), Heder (Antwerpen), GielsBos (Gierle), 't Zwart Goor (Merksplas). Roles: begeleider dagbesteding of atelierbegeleider; klasse 1 bachelor or klasse 2 with secondary diploma. Fit 3 to 4. Vacancies read "bachelor in een sociale of paramedische richting, of A2 met relevante ervaring"; a maker with real craft skill is attractive because most social-work graduates cannot weld or do joinery.
- Vlaamse Sociale Kaart (desocialekaart.be): official directory to build a target list by gemeente and type.

## 5. Trainings that open these doors in 1 to 2 years (Antwerp)
- Graduaat Orthopedagogische begeleiding (AP, KdG, Thomas More): 120 ECTS, 2 years full-time or 3 years werk- of avondtraject, one third werkplekleren; opvoeder-begeleider is knelpunt and the graduaat likely OKOT-eligible (UNVERIFIED 2026-27). Opens klasse 1 pay, atelierbegeleider, NAFT.
- Graduaat Sociaal-cultureel werk or Maatschappelijk werk: 120 ECTS, often werktraject; Antwerp availability UNVERIFIED; probably not OKOT.
- Jeugd- en gehandicaptenzorg (CVO, secundair volwassenenonderwijs): 1,5 to 2 years evening; moves klasse 2B to 2A.
- Educatief graduaat secundair onderwijs (AP, KdG, Thomas More): 90 ECTS, flexible or evening; entry with diploma secundair plus 3 years of relevant experience; LIO-baan allows paid part-time teaching during study; knelpunt; opens praktijkleraar in BuSO OV3 or TSO/BSO metaal and instructor roles.
- Bachelor Ergotherapie (AP): 3 years day; opens atelier leadership in psychiatry and VAPH.
- Fietshersteller (VDAB): weeks to months, free for jobseekers, knelpunt.
- Natuurbeheer courses (Inverde). Bio-ecologisch bouwen (VIBE, SYNTRA). Monitor sociale economie (Herwin, Groep Maatwerk, VIVO).
Reminder: any VDAB-funded route presumes dismissal by the employer and VDAB registration first (onderzoek 1).
