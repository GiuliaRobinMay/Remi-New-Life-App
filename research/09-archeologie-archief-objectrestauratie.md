## 1. Archaeology
- Organisation (BACKGROUND): since 2016 Flemish archaeology is "developer pays" under the Onroerenderfgoeddecreet: archeologienota by an erkend archeoloog, vooronderzoek, opgraving, all under the Code van Goede Praktijk of Onroerend Erfgoed. Nearly all fieldwork is done by private firms. Erkenning (type 1 and 2) requires a master plus field experience: out of reach within 3 years, and not needed for the roles below.
- Roles without a master: veldtechnicus or veldmedewerker (digs, cleans and photographs profiles, draws sections, measures with total station or GNSS, sieves, samples, packs finds), vondstverwerker (washes, labels, sorts, inventories), depotmedewerker (storage, climate, registers, loans), erkende metaaldetectorist in the team (the Code prescribes metal detection of every trench). Conservation of archaeological metal (stabilising iron and copper alloys, desalination, corrosion removal under the microscope) directly extends a welder's material knowledge; done in the agency's conservatielabo, a few depots and museums (Gallo-Romeins Museum Tongeren, Raakvlak Brugge, Erfpunt), often outsourced to KIK-IRPA. Formal entry: the metaal specialisation of Conservatie-Restauratie.
- Erkenning metaaldetectorist (BACKGROUND, details UNVERIFIED): free, online at Onroerend Erfgoed, for adults; obligations: landowner permission, daylight, limited depth, never on protected or researched sites, finds registered on MEDEA (vondsten.be). The cheapest credential Remi can add this autumn and a real asset on a veldtechnicus application.
- Employers (hiring UNVERIFIED): BAAC Vlaanderen, Ruben Willaert, All-Archeo (Bornem), RAAP België, ADEDE, Monument Vandekerckhove archeologie, Studiebureau Archeologie, ARON, GATE, Fenikx, ABO (Aartselaar), Antea Group (Antwerpen), Hembyse, J. Verrijckt (Beerse); public: Erfpunt (Waasland, depot), SOLVA, IOED Erfgoed Noorderkempen, IOED Zuidrand and other IOEDs, Stad Antwerpen dienst archeologie, Agentschap Onroerend Erfgoed depot and conservatielabo (a 2024 depotmedewerker vacancy at C121 asked only a diploma hoger secundair, CONFIRMED).
- Training: bachelor Archeologie at UGent and KU Leuven, VUB (kunstwetenschappen en archeologie), 3 years, open admission; master required for erkenning. No VDAB, CVO or SYNTRA veldtechnicus course; learned on site. Volunteering: AVRA (Antwerpse Vereniging voor Romeinse Archeologie), Archeologiedagen (May), Erfgoeddag.
- Pay (UNVERIFIED): veldtechnicus start about €2.300 to €2.700 bruto, project contracts, seasonal peaks. Boards: FARO, Archeonet Vlaanderen, VONA, agency vacatures, VDAB.

## 2. Archives and collection management
- Roles (BACKGROUND): archiefmedewerker (niveau C with diploma secundair for handling and retrieval; niveau B with graduaat or bachelor for description), depotmedewerker and depotbeheerder, collectieregistrator or registrar (usually bachelor or master), medewerker behoud en beheer, digitaliseringsmedewerker (niveau C), art handler (private firms Katoen Natie Art, Hasenkamp, Mobull, Hizkia Van Kralingen, UNVERIFIED), museumtechnicus. Depot, art handling, digitisation and technician roles are the quiet, precise, hands-on entry for a welder with a furniture background.
- Where (Antwerp first): FelixArchief, Letterenhuis, Erfgoedbibliotheek Hendrik Conscience, MAS and its collectiedepot, KMSKA depot, M HKA, FOMU, MoMu, Museum Plantin-Moretus, DIVA (silver, so metal), Middelheimmuseum; Stad Antwerpen C1-technisch assistent €2.460 without experience, €2.769 with 5 years (CONFIRMED). ADVN (Antwerpen), Rijksarchief Antwerpen-Beveren (federal, niveau C = diploma hoger secundair, CONFIRMED), KADOC (Leuven), Amsab-ISG and Liberas (Gent), meemoo (Gent), Vlaamse Erfgoedbibliotheken, Erfgoedcel Antwerpen.
- Training: Graduaat Informatiebeheer: bibliotheek en archief, 120 studiepunten, 2 years (3 spread), offered by Arteveldehogeschool (Gent, blended or evening option) and Erasmushogeschool Brussel (BACKGROUND, 2026 UNVERIFIED); whether AP Antwerpen offers it: UNVERIFIED (hogeronderwijsregister.be). The only sub-bachelor diploma that opens niveau B archive work. Master Archivistiek (VUB) is a master-na-master. Bachelor Geschiedenis (UAntwerpen) suits the profile but leads to niveau A only with a master. Short courses: FARO (behoud en beheer, depotwerking, registratie), Depotwijzer, Histories vzw.

## 3. Object restoration
- UAntwerpen Conservatie-Restauratie (Campus Mutsaard, same campus as the Academie): open to anyone with a diploma secundair, no artistic toelatingsproef mentioned (CONFIRMED; onderzoek 7 disagreed, so ask UAntwerpen directly); specialisations glas, keramiek, hout, schilderkunst, muurschilderkunst, metaal, steen, beeldende media, textiel, papier; ateliers for metaal and steen en polychromie (CONFIRMED). 180 plus 60 studiepunten (UNVERIFIED). Werkstudentenstatuut exists but atelier courses need daytime presence. EVK for KASKA credits decided per file (UNVERIFIED).
- KIK-IRPA (Brussels, federal): ateliers for metaal, steen en beeldhouwwerk, hout en meubel, schilderijen, textiel, glas-in-lood, papier, plus laboratories (BACKGROUND). Technical, photographic, logistics and depot posts at niveau B/C via werkenvoor.be.
- IPARC (Kampenhout): largest private conservation company in Belgium; paintings, polychrome sculpture, paper, contemporary art, preventive conservation, art handling; regularly hires and takes interns (BACKGROUND). Metal atelier in 2026: UNVERIFIED.
- Private ateliers: use the BRK-APROA member directory (searchable by specialisation: metaal, meubel, uurwerken, archeologisch materiaal) and the vakmanschap pages of Onroerend Erfgoed rather than unverified names. Restoration contractors with metalwork on monuments: Verstraete & Vanhecke, Monument Group, Renotec, Artes Woudenberg, Denys, Vandenbussche. Bronze foundry with restoration work: Art Casting, Oudenaarde (UNVERIFIED).
- In-house museum conservation: KMSKA, MAS, M HKA, Middelheimmuseum (outdoor sculpture in bronze and steel: the clearest place where welding meets conservation ethics), Erfgoedlab Antwerpen (collection care and 3D digitisation, status UNVERIFIED).

## 4. History and heritage mediation with a technical angle
- Erfgoedbewaker, zaalwachter: niveau D/C, quiet and stable, €2.100 to €2.400 (UNVERIFIED), no craft content: bridge job at most.
- Gids, museumeducator: public-facing all day; poor fit.
- Erfgoedconsulent bouwkundig erfgoed: bachelor or master; target after a degree.
- Monumentenwachter: inspects roofs, towers and interiors (an interieur team checks furniture, silver, bells and sculpture in churches), writes precise condition reports; excellent match; usual profile bachelor plus werken op hoogte; "door ervaring" UNVERIFIED.
- Technisch en bouwkundig tekenaar erfgoed: restoration architects and bouwhistorisch onderzoek firms need people who measure buildings, draw as-built plans and details of joinery and metalwork, process laser scans; Antwerp-area names to check (UNVERIFIED): Bureau Bouwtechniek, Erfgoed & Visie, Examino, Architectenbureau Karel Breda; elsewhere Origin, Callebaut Architecten, Fenikx, Studiebureau Monumentenzorg. Matching diploma: Graduaat Bouwkundig tekenen (AP, Thomas More), likely knelpunt.
- 3D scanning erfgoed; maquettebouw and exhibition fabrication (Meyvaert, Potteau, Bruns).

## 5. Combining study with unemployment benefits
- Ground rules (CONFIRMED in 01 and 04): 24-month cap; a full-time VDAB-recognised beroepsopleiding of at least 3 months started after 1 January 2026 can extend the right by up to 12 months; whether a regular bachelor with vrijstelling counts is UNVERIFIED. Vrijstelling: at least 20 hours a week daytime, at least 27 new studiepunten; benefits continue. Historically about one year of unemployment before a non-knelpunt study (2026 status UNVERIFIED). Timeline: if an 18-week employer notice covers Remi to early May 2027, by September 2027 he has drawn benefits for about four months, so a one-year rule would block a vrijstelling for 2027-2028 unless the study is knelpunt-linked or an OKOT. This is the single most important question for VDAB.
- Programme by programme: bachelor Archeologie, Conservatie-Restauratie, Geschiedenis: daytime, 60 studiepunten, pass the test, none knelpunt, none OKOT; vrijstelling depends on the bemiddelaar. Graduaat Informatiebeheer: daytime trajectory passes; evening variant does not qualify; not knelpunt. Graduaat Bouwkundig tekenen: probable knelpunt link and OKOT potential (benefits kept, tuition paid by VDAB).
- Costs: studiegeld 2026-2027 about €1.200 to €1.260 for 60 studiepunten, roughly €130 beursstudent, about €600 bijna-beursstudent (UNVERIFIED). Studietoelage: a 20-year-old who has lived a year on his own wage is assessed as zelfstandig student on his own income; when income falls, an assessment on the vermoedelijk inkomen of the current year can be requested (UNVERIFIED).

## 6. Networks and first steps (autumn 2026 to spring 2027)
1. Apply for the erkenning metaaldetectorist (free) and join AVRA.
2. Volunteer one fixed day a week: FelixArchief or Letterenhuis, MAS or KMSKA, Rijksarchief indexing (DEMOGEN), or an industrial-heritage workshop where welding is welcome (Stoomtrein Dendermonde-Puurs, Stoomcentrum Maldegem, Industriemuseum Gent, maritime heritage Antwerp).
3. Calendar: Archeologiedagen (May 2027), Erfgoeddag (18 April 2027), UAntwerpen C-R and AP infodagen (spring).
4. Weekly boards: FARO, ICOM Belgium Flanders, VVBAD, Archeonet, werkenvoorvlaanderen.be, werkenvoor.be, job.antwerpen.be, VDAB. Read Ex situ and the Onroerend Erfgoed newsletter.
5. Ask VDAB in writing before 31 December: vrijstelling conditions for a non-knelpunt bachelor in 2027; OKOT status of Graduaat Bouwkundig tekenen; whether a bachelor with vrijstelling extends the 24-month cap.
6. Beroepsverkennende stage in a museum depot, Middelheim conservation or a restoration contractor; propose an IBO in spontaneous applications.

## Ranked shortlist
1. Bachelor Conservatie-Restauratie, specialisatie metaal (UAntwerpen): strongest fit; welding knowledge becomes conservation of metal objects, sculpture and archaeological finds; 3 plus 1 years; vrijstelling uncertain; start September 2027 after a year of volunteering and a depot job.
2. Depot, art-handling and technical-assistant jobs at Antwerp institutions now (C1, €2.460 to €2.770): the best income-first route, combinable with evening study.
3. Graduaat Bouwkundig tekenen via OKOT, aimed at restoration architects, bouwhistorisch onderzoek and Monumentenwacht: best financial construction.
4. Graduaat Informatiebeheer: bibliotheek en archief (daytime for vrijstelling): archiefmedewerker or depotbeheerder at FelixArchief, ADVN, Letterenhuis, Rijksarchief, meemoo.
5. Veldtechnicus or vondstverwerker in commercial archaeology plus erkenning metaaldetectorist and AVRA: a real test before committing to a bachelor; weaker on stability.
6. Museum exhibition and showcase fabrication (Meyvaert, Potteau, Bruns): immediate use of TIG/MIG for museums; can fund option 1 or 4.
7. Metal restauratievakman via restoration contractors and industrial heritage volunteering.
8. Bachelor Archeologie: open admission, but the job market requires a master plus erkenning; only after option 5 confirms the attraction.
Not shortlisted: Bachelor Geschiedenis, Master Archivistiek, gids and educator roles, erfgoedbewaker (bridge only).
